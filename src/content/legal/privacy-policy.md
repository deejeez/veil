# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** June 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Matthew Giancristofaro ("Veil," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use Veil, our wedding planning application available at getwed.ai and app.getwed.ai (the "Service").

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information you provide directly

- **Account information.** When you create an account, we collect your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored in hashed form. We never see or store your password in plain text.
- **Wedding planning details.** Information you enter to plan your wedding, including your wedding date, style or vibe preferences, budget, guest count, and city.
- **Vendor information.** Vendor names, contacts, notes, and communication logs you record in the Service.
- **Payment tracking data.** Amounts and dates you enter to track your own vendor payments. This is information you type in for your own planning. It does not involve us moving money on your behalf.
- **Uploaded documents.** Contracts, proposals, and other files you upload. These files may contain personal information about you and about third parties such as vendors. You are responsible for ensuring you have the right to upload them.

### Payment information for your Veil purchase

When you purchase access to Veil, our payment processor, Stripe, collects and processes your payment card information directly. We do not store your full card number. We receive limited confirmation details such as the fact that a payment succeeded and the last four digits of your card.

### Information collected automatically

- **Usage and device data.** Basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, and how you interact with the Service. We use this to operate, secure, and improve the Service.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the Service.
- Process your purchase and provide customer support.
- Run the AI features of the Service, including contract review, proposal extraction, and planning guidance.
- Communicate with you about your account, updates, and support requests.
- Detect, prevent, and address security issues, fraud, and abuse.
- Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. AI Processing

A core part of Veil uses artificial intelligence to analyze the content you provide. When you upload a contract or proposal, or use features that analyze your entered data, the relevant content is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, through its commercial API for processing.

Anthropic processes this content solely to return the analysis to you. Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, the data sent through the API is not used to train AI models.

AI analysis can contain errors. You should always verify any extracted figures, dates, or terms against your original documents. Veil's AI features are a planning aid and are not a substitute for professional legal or financial advice.

## 4. How We Share Information

We share information only in the following cases:

- **Service providers.** We use third parties to operate the Service. These include:
  - **Supabase** for database, authentication, and file storage.
  - **Stripe** for payment processing.
  - **Anthropic** for AI processing, as described above.
  - **Vercel** and **Framer** for hosting.
  
  Each provider receives only the information needed to perform its function and is bound to protect it.
- **Legal requirements.** We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of Veil, our users, or others.
- **Business transfers.** If Veil is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. When you delete your account, we delete your associated personal data, subject to any legal obligations to retain certain records. Some information may persist in backups for a limited period before being overwritten.
